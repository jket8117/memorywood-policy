English

  • Memory Wood does not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email, or phone number.
  • Your progress — currencies, restored memories, and settings — is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere.
  • There is no sign-in and no advertising. Payments are not connected yet.
  • Notifications are scheduled by your device alone. No server is involved and you can turn them off in Settings at any time.
  • Deleting the app also removes the progress stored on your device.
